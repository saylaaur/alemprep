The assignment was rolled back, and all synthetic users/school/group/memberships were removed with normal FK checks.

Observed: `{revocationCommittedBeforePublish:true, publicationAccepted:true}`. Requests begun after revocation are already rejected; the uncovered case is an overlapping authorized request. Apply the same authorization-row treatment to invite creation, cancellation and invite issuer checks where they use this relationship. This completes the intended transactional revocation guarantee; it does not imply arbitrary students can publish.
