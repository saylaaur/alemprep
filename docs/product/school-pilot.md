# School pilot

AlemPrep supports independent mathematics practice. A teacher chooses a topic and shares the invitation. Students practise on their own accounts, and the teacher reviews class activity and server-confirmed answers.

The October 2026 release offered 89 bilingual problem families across four topics. The October rehearsal verified the workflow with test accounts. Treat that record as a test of the workflow and use live school records for adoption metrics. The pilot material went through AI-assisted review. Record any separate human review before describing content as teacher-reviewed.

## First use

1. Confirm a participating teacher, class, language, suitable topic, and slot for the first session. A teacher and coordinator sign in before an operator binds their accounts to a school. Use the [operator bootstrap template](../../supabase/pilot/bootstrap-first-class.sql) and keep the filled copy private.
2. The teacher opens the [Kazakh cabinet](https://alemprep.vercel.app/kk/teacher) or its Russian equivalent, selects their class, and creates an invitation. The locale of the open cabinet determines the language of the invitation link. A code lasts up to 72 hours and accepts up to 100 joins.
3. One pupil opens the invitation, signs in, joins the class, and completes onboarding if prompted. The pupil answers one problem and reloads after the result saves.
4. The teacher refreshes the report and checks that one new confirmed attempt appears. The teacher can download a CSV to confirm the same totals. After that check, invite a small initial group and agree on the next session with the teacher.

Measure the number of pupils who join, submit a first answer, and practise again on another day. Keep team rehearsals separate from school usage. Send aggregate results outside the class when appropriate. Keep identifiable reports with authorised school staff.
